# OrangeStorm Giga — BTT SFS V2.1 mount

The tested installation has always used a BTT SFS V2.1, as confirmed by its owner on 11 October 2026. Earlier V2.0 labels in the installation notes were incorrect. This does not establish universal compatibility between revisions.

## Exact models

- [STL](orangestorm-giga-btt-sfs-v2.1-mount-remix.stl)
- [Model-only 3MF](orangestorm-giga-btt-sfs-v2.1-mount-remix.3mf)

Both are the unchanged binary files from the final September hardware pack, renamed for the correct installation identity. No geometry was generated or converted. The 3MF contains only its content types, relationship and model XML, not private slicer profiles. It is not parametric CAD. See [provenance and SHA-256](../PROVENANCE.json).

## Assembly

The owner's 13 September record specifies 3 × M3 × 16 mm + 3 M3 washers, 2 × M4 × 10 mm + 2 M4 washers, black PVC 3-conductor 22 AWG cable, and 3-pin male/female JST-XH connectors marketed as 2.54 mm. Verify the actual mating connector and physical SIGNAL/GND/+5 V order, not the housing orientation.

See the [complete hardware guide](../docs/hardware/BTT-SFS-V2.1-ORANGESTORM-GIGA.md).
